# ClaudeRobot — build specification v2.1

One MT5 Expert Advisor holding the ten validated rules, the three changes kept by the 2022-2025 look (behind switches), their sizing and their protections, for FP Markets (Raw, `.r` symbols, hedging, 1:500) on the MetaQuotes VPS. New code only, in `lab/robot/` (`lab/robot/spike/` is read-only). Lab data in sample only: price frames and trades are read with a filter that stops before 2022-01-01 inside the read call (`pd.read_parquet(path, filters=[("ts", "<", CUT)])`; never `r2.load`, which reads the whole file first); `NUMBA_CACHE_DIR` under /tmp; `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`; never `lab/holdout_raw`, `lab/data/holdout`, `results/holdout*` or any 2026 file. Generated files (bar exports, tables, builds, binaries) go to `lab/robot/check/out/` or `lab/robot/build/`, both git-ignored. v2 answers the two attacks of `design_review.json` (P1-P10, R1-R16) and the lead's four decisions (L1-L4); v2.1 answers the independent review of the built file (`build_reports_part2.json` key `review`, F1-F12) and the user's stake plan D2 (PROTOCOL_V3 Addendum G, last notes); §11 lists each change.

## 0. Principles

1. A **paper chain** per sleeve: `goldlab/sim.py` run bar by bar on the broker's closed M1 bars, with the lab's quotes (§2.2). It alone decides entries, the one-position rule and exits; account skips never change it. When the lab places an exit before a gap that it can see in its data, the paper books that exit as soon as the gap is seen (§2.5); this never changes which trades the chain takes.
2. The account **follows**: market orders only; every robot position carries a broker stop at the paper stop (§4.4); it closes when the paper exits.
3. **No persistent state**: every start and every bar revision rebuilds everything from the broker: bars, deals, orders, positions. The protections are rebuilt from the deal history; the disaster guard writes its own mark into it (§6.2).
4. **Proof by replay**: the shipped file, translated to C++, replays in-sample bars against the lab code (spike: brk72 BTC 632 of 645 trades bit for bit; the 13 others were lab exits before data holes, which the v2 paper books like the lab).

VPS, checked (mql5.com/en/vps/rules, MT5 migration help, MQL5 article 994): no DLL; EAs migrate with their chart, scripts do not; ≤ 500,000 bars; restarts for updates and maintenance (up to 15 min a day on weekdays, 4 h a day at weekends); global variables not migrated, sync one-way (files written there count as lost, forum 363057); `TERMINAL_VPS` flags it; one EA may trade four symbols (ClaudeMains.mq5:56).

## 2. Conventions

### 2.1 Time (CR_Clock)
- UTC seconds inside. Server = NY + 7 h: UTC = server − 3 h if the US is on summer time at (server − 3 h), else − 2 h (pont.py:37-43); NY wall clock = server − 7 h exactly. US summer time: 2nd Sunday of March 07:00 UTC to 1st Sunday of November 06:00 UTC; UK: last Sundays of March and October, 01:00 UTC.
- Server week from Monday 00:00 (stake.py:89-91, 131); the 17:00-NY trading day is the server date (data.py:45-49).
- `AddTradingDays(t, n)`: n business days later at the same server time; a weekend start counts Monday as day 1 (pandas BDay, trend/strategy.py:107-111). `BusinessDays(date, n)`: pandas `BDay(n)` on a calendar date, no holidays (H2: variants.py:22-23; ORX: orb/strategy.py:149).
- NYSE calendar: port of `us_holidays` (idxus_lib.py:53-83: full days and 13:00 half days, rule-based for any year, special closures through 2025-01-09) plus the typed `NYSE` and `HALF` entries.
- **Known closures** (NY clock), per family; an earlier broker session end (`SymbolInfoSessionTrade`) wins:
  - XAU: daily 17:00-18:00; Friday 17:00 to Sunday 18:00; the NYSE's observed Good Friday, Christmas and New Year: [day before 17:00, day 18:00) (no gold bar on any of them in 2013-2021); the other NYSE full holidays: [13:00, 18:00) (last in-sample gold bar 12:00-14:16 on all but one day, 12:59-13:00 on most; a few scattered bars to 16:56 on 2019-07-04); the Friday after Thanksgiving and a Christmas Eve half day: [13:45, 18:00) (last bar 13:29-14:05, mostly 13:43-13:44). July 3 half days and 2018-12-05 are normal gold days.
  - US100: daily 16:15-18:00; Friday 16:15 to Sunday 18:00.
  - Crypto: Friday 16:55 to Sunday 17:00 (server Friday 23:55 to Monday 00:00).
  - Typed `InpExtraClosures` (P4): entries separated by `;`, each `NYSE yyyy.mm.dd` (a full NYSE closure), `HALF yyyy.mm.dd` (an NYSE 13:00 half day), or `XAU|IDX|CRY yyyy.mm.dd hh:mm-yyyy.mm.dd hh:mm` (a closure window of that market, NY clock). The lab writes this list (§10.5).
- `RealExitMinute(x, family)` (replaces v1's `ExitMinute`; used by the follower only, §4.5): for a rule with the gap rule, x inside a known closure [c0, c1) with x < c1 − 30 min → c0 − 1 min; crypto: never after the broker's Friday session end − 1 min; else x. The paper does not use the calendar (§2.5).

### 2.2 Bars, quotes, barrier, depth (CR_Bars)
- Names by `Resolve` (ClaudeMains.mq5:206-226). M1 only, read by time range (`CopyRates(sym, PERIOD_M1, from, to)`), never by position, which loses a just-closed bar (map `mains` pitfall 0). Chart mode must be bid.
- Bar = UTC start, broker bid O/H/L/C, raw spread s (points).
- **Quotes = the lab's FP model (L3, P7).** Paper bid = `NormalizeDouble(bid − h, digits)`, paper ask = `NormalizeDouble(bid + s × point + h, digits)` on all four prices; mid = (paper bid + paper ask)/2, which h does not move (build_fp_frames.py:41-45). h = half the round-trip commission per unit: XAU `CR_XAU_COMM_PTS`/2 = 3 points = 0.03 USD/oz (FP Raw charges 3 USD per side per 100-oz lot; the lab's 0.19 USD = 0.13 spread + 0.06 commission, COSTS_FP.md:8, fp_costs.json); US100, BTC, ETH 0 (no commission on Raw). Rules, paper and sizing see only paper quotes. Why the broker's raw spread plus a fixed commission, not the lab's level in bp: the 0.13 USD of the model was an estimate of this very raw spread, and keeping the broker's own quotes makes paper and broker stops trigger on the same tick (§4.4). At 4,080 USD both give 0.19 USD; at 3,000 USD the paper carries 1.6 points more than the lab's 0.147 bp commission, at 5,000 USD 1.4 points less (§9.13). The raw spread is journaled beside every paper quote it built (L3, §2.6).
- **Mask**, counted: server weekend (prep_round2.py:53); crypto from Friday 23:55 server (build_fp_frames.py:31-33); US100 16:15-17:59 NY; XAU 17:00-17:59 NY; **US100 on NYSE half days from 13:16 to 17:59 NY** (P6: in-sample half days end at 13:00-13:15). The bars left are the kept bars. **No bid-flat rule** (B1): MT5 makes a bar only when a tick came, so it has no filler bars; the lab's rule (prep_round2.py:49) ran on Dukascopy's 3-decimal bid, and on the 2-digit FP bid it dropped 5,969 real in-sample bars that the rebuilt frames keep. Kept bars = rebuilt frames: `export_frames.py` drops the gold bars of 17:00-17:59 NY (in sample the 20 bars of 2012-12-31 17:00-17:58, inside the daily closure), so the references run on exactly the bars the EA keeps (§11, X1). A minute M whose bar the mask would drop, or that lies in a known closure of the symbol's family, is **shut** (`BarsShut`): no order is sent in it (§4.1).
- **Barrier M**: every symbol has ticked at or after M, or `TimeTradeServer()` ≥ M + 2 s, or is in a known closure; bars before M are then final. A minute that ticked but whose bar is not returned is retried 30 s, then `GAP`. The last 3 bars are re-read each minute; a change → `LATEBAR` → rebuild (§4.8), at most one per 5 min.
- **Depth and READY** (R13): XAU 120 days, others 60 (twice every measured warm-up and chain resync: maps `us100` 8, `ml` 8, `leadlag` 11, `or-seances` 7, `or-tendance` 18-19; donch ATR within 4e-15 at 120 days; about 120,000 gold bars). A symbol is READY when (1) `SeriesInfoInteger(sym, PERIOD_M1, SERIES_SYNCHRONIZED)` is true, (2) its history covers the depth, (3) two reads 2 s apart agree and (4) no hole over 30 min lies inside its sessions (known closures excluded). A hole → NOT_READY, `GAP` push, re-read every 60 s; a hole still there after 3 re-reads with SERIES_SYNCHRONIZED true is the broker's own (`GAP accepted=1`) and the symbol becomes READY. Until then its sleeves are NOT_READY. The first check runs 2 s after each load (start, `LATEBAR`, reconnect), in the timer pass before the follower (§4.8); `BarsNotReadyFor` tells the follower whether that first check has decided and how long the symbol has been NOT_READY since.
- Daily cost statistics, per symbol and server day: median raw spread and median paper spread, in bp of the mid (§2.6 `COST`).

### 2.4 Rule interface
One class per rule, one object per sleeve, `switch(kind)` dispatch (no virtual calls): `Init(sleeve)` (frozen constants, each with its file:line); `OnBar(sym, const CrBar &b, CrOut &out)` for each kept closed bar of its symbols, in time order (ties by symbol id), **before** the paper engine; `OnMinute(M, CrOut &out)` after barrier M, on bars before M only; `LatestExit(open_time, comment_dec)`: the latest time exit consistent with a position opened at open_time (§4.8 table; the comment's decision is used only when it parses and lies inside the rule's entry window before open_time).

`CrOrder`: sleeve, oid (decision × 100 + id), side, etype (0 market, 1 stop, 2 limit, 3 OCO), level, level2; activation = window [t_from, t_to] (first kept bar starting inside) or `after_exit_of` an oid (first kept bar after its exit bar and before t_to; void with it); expiry_excl; stop or stop_dist; trail, be, target (0 = none); x_sched and its gap flag (§2.5); `xh`, `xh_r` (hold extension; 0 = none); `refuse` (0 none, 1 payoff, 2 sB, §2.5); `void_le` (void when the exit bar does not follow the entry bar); dec; group. `CrExit`: oid + window, exit at the first kept bar inside (void if none). Rules keep the lab's own validity conditions; READY only gates real orders.

### 2.5 Paper engine (CR_Paper)
Port of sim.py `_run` (:69-211), fed after the rule's `OnBar`, for each kept closed bar b: (1) the open trade — time exit at b's open if b is its exit bar and not the fill bar (:137), then stop (at the stop on the fill bar, later at the worse of stop and open; reason `trail` once moved), then target, then best, trail and break-even for the next bar (:136-204); (2) orders activating on b — skipped if the trade is open or exited on b (:80); fills per :87-111; OCO with both levels touched → the side against the bar's move, bc ≥ bo → short (trend/strategy.py:303-317); stop absolute or fill ∓ dist, a wrong-side stop exits on the fill bar as `gap` (:117-127); the fill bar is then managed as in (1). Stamps: entry = fill bar start; exit = exit bar start, + 60 s unless `time` (:283-284). ss, sm = fp_costs.json slip_stop, slip_mkt bps × 1e-4 (XAU 1.0/0.3, US100 0.5/0.2, BTC 1.5/0.5, ETH 2.0/0.7). Ties keep emission order. Two pending orders of one sleeve at once = `ERROR` (it cannot occur in the ten rules). No swap, no end-of-data exit.
- **Exit bar.** Without the gap flag: the first kept bar starting at or after x. With it (gl.exit_index, data.py:95-104): the first kept bar starting in [x, x + 30 min]; if none starts there, the last kept bar before x — or, when that bar is the fill bar, the first kept bar after x (sim.py:137 `i > fi`).
- **Late gap revision (P4).** The engine keeps, for each open trade, a copy of its state from before its last processed bar. When the barrier passes x + 30 min (every bar starting at or before x + 30 min is final) and no kept bar starts in [x, x + 30 min], it restores that copy and books the time exit at that bar's open (`PREVISE`, replacing a stop or trail exit booked on that bar); when that bar is the fill bar nothing is restored and the exit waits for the first kept bar after x, unless the order has `void_le`. With `void_le` (brk72's wrapper), an order whose exit bar does not follow its entry bar never existed: checked at activation (x at or before the entry bar's start: it does not fill) and at a late revision whose bar is the entry bar (`PVOID`). The revision never changes the chain: no kept bar lies between the two candidate bars, and it is booked before the next kept bar is processed, so an order activating there finds the sleeve free, as in the lab. Known closures need no special case: the paper reads bars and time only.
- **Hold extension (H2).** At the first barrier at or after x, for an open trade with xh > 0: if its last processed bar c is at or after the fill bar and side × (mark − entry) ≥ xh_r × risk, with mark = c's paper bid close (long) or ask close (short), entry = paper fill, risk = |entry − initial stop|, then x := max(xh, x) (`HOLD`; variants.py:206-233). A late gap revision re-runs the test on the restored state.
- **3-spread refusal**, at the fill on bar i, spread = ao_i − bo_i: refuse 1 (payoff lens): d0 < 3 × spread, d0 = stop_dist for distance stops, else |fp − stop| (variants.py:176-178); refuse 2 (synthesis_B): |fp − st| < 3 × spread, or st on the wrong side (synthesis_B/common.py:136-169). A refused order leaves no trace: no trade, the sleeve stays flat, no "exited on b" (`PREFUSE`). This equals the lab's removal and re-simulation because the test depends on the order alone.
- Events `PFILL`, `PEXIT`, `PSTOP`, `PREVISE`, `PVOID`, `PREFUSE`, `HOLD`.

### 2.6 Journal
`CR|<utc>|<EVENT>|<code>|k=v|…` (utc = lab time of the event) to `Print` and `MQL5/Files/ClaudeRobot/<yyyymmdd>.log` (30 days); doubles by `DoubleToString(x,-16)` (exact round trip). Lines written while a rebuild replays the history carry `rp=1` and go to the file only, never to `Print` (about 4,000 per rebuild); the file is flushed once per timer pass, not line by line (F9). The VPS keeps no file the user can fetch (§0): its experts log, which holds the live lines, is the VPS copy's journal. Events: INIT, SELFTEST, PLAN, READY, NOTREADY, DEC (with skip=K1 riskbp=…), PFILL, PEXIT, PSTOP, PREVISE, PVOID, PREFUSE, HOLD, SIZE, SEND, FILL, SLMOD, SLFIX, CLOSE, REFUSED, ADOPT, ORPHAN, DUP, MISSED, LATEBAR, GAP, REBUILD, SETTLE, CLOCK, STOP, GUARD, MARK, LOCK, COST, TAKEOVER, WATCH, ERROR. Every PFILL, SEND and FILL line carries `s_raw` (the broker's spread) and `s_paper` (L3). `COST`, daily per symbol: median raw and paper spread in bp against the lab model (fp_costs.json: XAU 0.47, US100 0.37, BTC 2.96, ETH 13.0 bp), and on gold the measured commission per oz from `DEAL_COMMISSION` against 0.06 USD. Pushes (French): STOP, GUARD, MARK while it fails, LOCK, CLOCK, ORPHAN, MISSED, REFUSED, SLFIX, DUP, ERROR, GAP, NOTREADY over 10 min, a GFP Friday skipped for the broker's gold session (F7), PLAN unset on a real account (daily), COST over 1.5 × the model or a commission 1 point off, K1 with no T for the year, TAKEOVER, WATCH, a daily summary, and two yearly reminders (1 December: next year's closures; 1 January: next K1 threshold).

## 4. Follower (CR_Follower, CR_Broker)

At barrier M, after `OnBar`, the paper and `OnMinute(M)`:
1. **Entries.** A market order becomes an intent when it activates (the symbol's first tick at or after t_from, in a minute ≤ t_to that is not shut, §2.2) and its sleeve's paper trade ended on an earlier bar (a trade with a pending gap exit has not ended); stamp = that minute. A shut minute (masked for the symbol, e.g. the crypto weekend, or inside a known closure) never activates an order, whatever the broker quotes: the paper cannot fill on a masked bar (F2; brk72 decides at Friday 21:00/22:00 UTC = Saturday 00:00 server, inside the crypto mask). An observer makes the same intents as the VPS copy with `InpTrade` false (sized `NOTRADE`, never sent), so the two shadow journals agree (§10.12, F6). A stop, limit or OCO order becomes an intent at its `PFILL` on bar i: stamp i, sent at barrier i+1 — **unless its paper trade has already exited** (stopped, `gap` or revised on its fill bar): no send, `MISSED reason=exited`; it still sets the week unit if it is the week's first sizing event (P5, R15; in sample 1 of 495 donch trades). **Decision** (unchanged): market one bar late, not broker pending orders or tick emulation, so real and paper never disagree on which trades exist. Intents are sized (§5) by stamp, then lab sleeve name in byte order (stake.py:108); sendable until stamp + 5 min (the lab's tolerance), then `MISSED`. **Just before every request** (the first and every retry) the entry blocks are read again — `STOPPED`, `GUARD`, `LOCK`, `FRIDAY`, `CLOCK`, the self-test, `NOTRADE`, and the sleeve's READY — and no request goes in a shut minute: the intent then waits inside its window and ends `MISSED` with that reason (`market_shut` for a shut minute). A block that came after the sizing never lets a position open (F4).
2. **Risk for sizing (P1).** quote_p = (quote + side × h) × (1 + side × sm), the quote being the ask (long) or bid (short). Market intents: r = stop_dist, or side × (quote_p − stop) for absolute stops. Stop, limit and OCO intents, sent one bar late: r = max(side × (paper fill − stop), side × (quote_p − stop)) (`SIZE late=1`). The post-fill check below compares the real risk with 1.25 × the budget sized on r, so the delay alone never closes a trade. In sample the larger risk applies to 16 of 495 donch and 20 of 1,039 orb trades (upper bound before lot rounding), 0 of 414 GFP; their lots shrink by the risk ratio (a listed T7b exception).
3. **Send** with the hands' DoOpen checks (ClaudeMains.mq5:760-860): tick ≤ 10 s old, hedging, Algo Trading on, `SYMBOL_ORDER_SL` accepted, volume floored to the step, notional ≤ 20 × balance, stop beyond `STOPS_LEVEL` on the right side, deviation 2 spreads; after the fill the stop is verified and the real money risk, (side × (fill − SL) + 2h) / tick_size × tick_value_loss × lots, must be ≤ 1.25 × the budget, else close (`REFUSED`). **One intent, one trade (R5):** before any send the robot reads `HistorySelect(0, TimeTradeServer() + 86400)` and requires, for the sleeve's magic: no open order in any state; no history order set up at or after the stamp that opened a position (its `ORDER_POSITION_ID` is its own ticket: MQL5's position identifier is the opening order's ticket) or may still do so (no position id yet and not REJECTED, CANCELED or EXPIRED) — closing orders do not count; no `DEAL_ENTRY_IN` deal at or after the stamp (an OUT deal of the previous trade does not count); no position. A position left by the previous trade is closed first; the new intent waits inside its window. After an unknown result (no retcode, timeout, disconnection) nothing is re-sent until the connection is up, the ledger is settled (§5) and two reads of positions and orders 2 s apart agree; the checks above then decide; past the window → `MISSED`.
4. **Stops.** Broker stop = `GridStop(paper stop + side × h)`: rounded on the tick grid away from the market, by less than one tick (SpikeRobot.mq5:48-66). A raw quote touches it exactly when the paper quote touches the paper stop, and the real loss with commission equals the paper risk. Distance stops start from the quote and move to the paper stop at `PFILL`; `PSTOP` moves it again; a modification refused for `STOPS_LEVEL` or `FREEZE_LEVEL` keeps the old stop and is retried every 10 s (the paper's exit still closes the position); a stop the price has already crossed → close. **Invariant (R4)**, every timer pass, every robot position, NOT_READY sleeves included: SL > 0, on the right side, and for a READY sleeve equal to the expected stop. Different → `PositionModify`; no SL 5 s after `POSITION_TIME` (ClaudeMains.mq5:689), or a refused modify while SL = 0 → close and push (`SLFIX`). A position with no expected stop (NOT_READY or unfollowed sleeve) whose stop a fresh quote (at most 10 s old) has crossed for 2 min — the broker has not executed it — is closed and pushed (`SLFIX why=wrong_side`, F8); the 2 min leave the broker its own trigger (the mock's fast mode books stops at the bar's end).
5. **Exits** at the barrier of `RealExitMinute(x)` for the paper's pending time exit, and on `PEXIT`, `PREVISE`, `PVOID`. A real stop hit first, a `REFUSED` or a `MISSED` trade leaves the sleeve busy until the paper exits. Retries every 10 s, 60 s if the market is closed (Watch, ClaudeMains.mq5:698-739).
6. **Duplicates (R5).** At every barrier, at most one robot position per magic: a newer extra one is closed (`DUP`).
7. **Ownership**: positions with magic 20261201-20261289; 20261290 is the guard's mark (§6.2); deals attach by `DEAL_POSITION_ID` (broker stop deals may carry magic 0: RobotDeals, ClaudeMains.mq5:340-367).
8. **Startup and rebuild.** `OnInit` checks inputs, symbols and the instance lock, runs the self-test and starts the timer (100 ms; 1 s in the Strategy Tester, R12). `OnTimer` then (a) waits until connected, the ledger settled (§5), and two reads of positions and orders 2 s apart agree (R5); a symbol not yet READY (§2.2) keeps its sleeves NOT_READY; (b) rebuilds the ledger and the history latches (§5, §6); (c) replays the history minute by minute through rules, paper and dry sizing, orders off; (d) reconciles — a same-side robot position opened within 5 min of its sleeve's open paper trade's stamp → `ADOPT`, stop reset; any other → close (`ORPHAN`); duplicates → keep the oldest (`DUP`); a paper trade without position → nothing if its position already closed, an intent inside its send window, else `MISSED` or a sizing skip; (e) goes live. `LATEBAR`, a reconnection after more than 60 s, or an input change reruns (a)-(d). Every timer pass runs the READY checks (§2.2) before the follower. A NOT_READY sleeve enters nothing; its position keeps its stop and closes at `LatestExit(POSITION_TIME, comment)` (P9, R9), **but only once** its symbols have had their first READY check since the load and the sleeve has stayed NOT_READY for 10 minutes since (F1: every rebuild starts NOT_READY, about 2 s, about 3 min with an accepted hole; closing at once closed positions the reconcile would have adopted, 53 held F1E trades and 3 ORX trades in T6):

| BKB, BKE | D = o floored to the UTC hour (o − D ≤ 10 min) | min(D + 24 h, Friday 23:54 server) |

9. **One trading copy, pauses, watch (R10, R14).** On the VPS copy (`TERMINAL_VPS`), `InpTrade` = false blocks new entries only: exits, stop upkeep, the invariant and the protections still run. Off the VPS, a copy on a real account, or on a demo account with `InpTrade` false, is an observer: it sends nothing at all (a demo copy with `InpTrade` true trades anywhere, as in v1). An observer keeps a twin journal (its intents sized `NOTRADE` at activation like the VPS copy's with `InpTrade` false, §4.1) and pushes `WATCH` when a robot position outlives its paper exit or its `LatestExit` by more than 5 min (the VPS copy may be dead). The procedures that keep exactly one trading copy are §10.15 (migration) and §10.12 (shadow weeks). Instance lock: a `GlobalVariableTemp` holding a heartbeat (`GetTickCount64`) refreshed every pass, taken with `GlobalVariableSetOnCondition`; a lock not refreshed for 10 s is stale and may be taken over (`TAKEOVER` push); `OnDeinit` releases it.

## 5. Account (CR_Ledger, CR_Sizing)

- **Plan start (R3).** T = `InpPlanStart`. 0 on a real account → `OBSERVE` (no new entry; exits and stop upkeep still run; `PLAN` push every day); 0 on a demo or contest account → T = the first deal of the history (push). T still to come → `OBSERVE` until T. B(T) = `ACCOUNT_BALANCE` − Σ money (profit + swap + commission + fee, credit deals excluded as in ClaudeMains.mq5:586) of the deals at or after T; no history before T is needed. B(T) counts as one opening payment at T. `InpPaidIn` and `InpRestartAfter` are gone: T does both jobs (R3, R11).
- **Ledger** from `HistorySelect(0, TimeTradeServer() + 86400)` at startup, at each `OnTradeTransaction` and once a minute. After T: payments = `DEAL_TYPE_BALANCE` (amount in `DEAL_PROFIT`; A > 0 deposit, A < 0 withdrawal; Capital(), ClaudeMains.mq5:565-619); robot closes: one close event per robot position, money = Σ profit + swap + commission + fee of its deals, stamped like the lab (sim.py:283-284): SL, TP or SO exit in minute X → X + 60 s, EA close → X; everything else (manual trades, the guard's mark, charges, corrections) is other money at its deal time: in B, never in w.
- **Settle (R16; Capital's settle rule, P10).** The ledger is settled when B(T), computed as above, is the same in two reads 2 s apart. While it moves (a deal and the balance out of step): no sizing (intents wait inside their window), no STOP, GUARD or LOCK conclusion (`SETTLE`). After 60 s of movement the latest value is accepted and pushed.
- **At stamp S** (payments ≤ S, then closes < S: growth.py:189-215): balance B; **P_in** = B(T) + Σ deposits after T — gross, never lower after a withdrawal (R1); **ref** starts at B(T); a deposit A → ref = max(ref + A, B right after) (growth.py:212); a withdrawal A < 0 → ref = ref × B_after / B_before, so a withdrawal moves neither B/ref nor the risk rung (R1, policy written here).
- **Week unit**, set at the week's first ledger event (a close, a sizing request, or a `MISSED reason=exited` intent): rate(max(B_w, P_in,w))% × B_w (growth.py:274). w = robot close money stamped this week before S / unit; payments and manual trades never count.
- **m** = 1 if w ≤ 0, else min(1, max(0.25, w/4)) (stake.py:184-185; cushion_only :472-473).
- **Rate**: the risk table's pct at max(B, P_in), at most 10 % (D2 starts at 10 %; growth.py key 1); the notional cap (20 × balance, §4.3) and the margin check (below) are unchanged and decide alone whether a large lot can be sent.
- **Lots** = floor(B × rate(max(B, P_in)) × m × f / 100 / per_lot / step + 1e-9) × step (growth.py:296); per_lot = r × (tick_value_loss / tick_size), r per §4.2; f = 1, or while its switch is on the change's frozen equal-swing factor: H2 0.750978, S0.75r 0.771793 (synthesis_A.json proposed_validation_look item 7: "a passing change goes to the demo account staked at its equal-swing factor"); K1 has none.
- **Checks** in the lab's order (growth.py:297-309, account.py:104-113): guards (`STOPPED`, `GUARD`, `LOCK`, `FRIDAY`, `CLOCK`, `NOTREADY`, `OBSERVE`, `SETTLE`, `PREV_OPEN`), `MIN_LOT` (never rounded up), `CAP` (6 positions, or 2 per symbol, counting closes stamped ≥ S), `MARGIN` (Σ `OrderCalcMargin` + new > B). Skips never touch the paper.

## 6. Protections (CR_Guards; history latches in CR_Ledger)

1. **Plan stop STOP** (growth.py:245-252; F3S, F2S, F1S :75-76): when `InpStopFallPct` > 0, after each booked robot close, P_in ≥ `InpStopArmPaid` and B ≤ ref × (1 − `InpStopFallPct`/100) → close all, stopped for good. `InpStopFallPct` 0 (the default, D2 and S7) = no plan stop. Derived from the deal history at every rebuild, so it survives restarts. Arming is a latch: P_in never falls, so withdrawals never disarm (R1). Only a later `InpPlanStart` clears it.
2. **Disaster guard GUARD** (P3, R2, R6), only with `InpGuard` true (default false: D2 and S7; true in the F3S preset). With `InpGuard` false nothing latches, a mark already in the history is ignored and no mark is made. With ref > 0, it latches when (a) equity ≤ 50 % of ref, checked live every timer pass on a settled ledger; or (b) B ≤ 50 % of ref at any ledger event since T (history); or (c) the history holds a deal with magic 20261290 at or after T (its own mark). On latching, every robot position is closed with requests carrying magic 20261290; if no deal of that magic exists after T, one minimum lot is opened and closed on the first open market with magic 20261290 and a broker stop 1 % away (cost about 0.2 EUR), retried every 60 s until such a deal exists (`MARK`). After a restart, (c) keeps it latched even when equity is back above 50 % (T8). Only a later `InpPlanStart` clears it. **On in F3S, off in D2 (the user's decision, Addendum G, second choice):** under F3S it binds where the account has lost half, which the user then called unacceptable ("I cannot lose 50 % and wait a year", third clarification); R6 put that at about 2.5 % of half-edge three-year paths and 21.9 % if the rules are worthless. With D2 a fall of 50 % or more comes in 73.2 % (paper) / 88.3 % (half edge) of the validation-year paths, so the guard would stop D2 in most paths: the user chose speed and accepts the loss of the account, and the guard is switched off for D2 (and S7: 49.0 % paper / 74.9 % half edge, ninth clarification). The user is told again before real money (§10.13).
3. **Daily lock** (X stakes): the server day's booked robot money ≤ −X × rate × the day's first balance → flat until the next server day. Booked money only, so it is rebuilt from the history. Off by default: the plan has none, and the hands' 6 % lock fires early in most F2S paths (sixth review).
4. **Friday close**: when set, close all and block entries from that Friday server time to Monday. Off by default: it cuts donch's PF 1.53 → 1.40 and orb's 1.34 → 1.19 in sample (map `mains` 12).
5. **Clock** (R7, R12): |TimeTradeServer − TimeGMT − rule| > 120 s, checked at `OnInit`, every 10 minutes and at the first tick after any market reopening → no entries at once (`CLOCK`); a mismatch lasting 10 minutes → close the robot positions (their exits run on a wrong clock), push, stay flat until the offset matches again. Skipped under `MQL_TESTER`, where TimeGMT equals TimeTradeServer.
6. **Broker-stop invariant** (§4.4) and **instance lock** (§4.9).
7. From the hands 1.11, kept: Resolve, RiskMoney, ToTick (as GridStop), CloseAll, the open checks, RobotDeals, Capital's settle rule only (P10: the reference follows §5, not Capital's equity rule ClaudeMains.mq5:609, 627), MustClose's no-stop rule, Notify's limits, Watch's rhythm. Dropped: the bridge, heartbeat, TTL globals, InpResetLocks, the 6 % lock, the −50 % kill kept in globals and a file (replaced by 2), the 3-spread minimum stop (the ten rules have none in the lab; the three changes refuse on paper, §2.5), InpMaxRiskPct (it would cut F3S's 3 %).

## 9. Accepted deviations from the lab

1. D4H, ORX and GFP fill for real one bar after the paper fill; paper and sizing keep the lab fill, except that the lots of a late intent are sized on the larger of the paper risk and the quote risk (P1: 16 of 495 donch, 20 of 1,039 orb in sample).
2. The paper books every lab exit before a gap like the lab (§2.5). The real position closes before a known closure (§2.1), but after an unforeseen hole: the spike's 13 BTC exits of 645 are of that kind.
3. GFP's early-close filter is a calendar plus the broker session; it equals the lab's skip set in sample.
4. Real stops trigger on ticks, paper stops on bars with one spread each; the paper decides.
5. Ask = bid + the bar's raw spread (+ the gold commission) on FP's feed: only "EA = lab code on the broker's bars" is provable; razor-thin rules will differ from the Dukascopy-based lab (nq_btc keeps 58 % of its trades under 1 bp of noise, map `leadlag` 21). Raw spread spikes are not clipped (the lab clipped the spread shape at 6 × the day's median, build_fp_frames.py:37-38): a spike inside ORX's London range widens its levels and stop (or-seances pitfall 12; T8 world).
6. Live EUR tick values and `OrderCalcMargin` replace 0.89 and fp_costs.json; for absolute stops the expected fill comes from the quote.
7. Gold intents are sized one minute after their stamp (it matters only when caps or margin bind).
8. Incremental statistics within 1e-12 of the lab; razor decisions may differ.
9. After a restart, an entry is joined only within 5 min of its stamp.
10. The disaster guard is outside the plan: off under D2 and S7 (`InpGuard` false, the user's choice), on in the F3S preset, where it binds in about 2.5 % of half-edge paths (§6.2).
11. NYSE special closures after 2025-01-09 only through typed `InpExtraClosures`.
12. A late intent whose paper trade stopped on its fill bar is not sent: the lab books its −1 R, the account books nothing (P5; 1 of 495 donch in sample).
13. The gold commission is a fixed 0.06 USD per oz; the lab scaled it with the price (0.147 bp): equal at 4,080 USD, 1.6 points more at 3,000, 1.4 points less at 5,000.
14. K1's threshold is an input refitted by the lab each 1 January; a year without one has K1 off.
15. A clock mismatch lasting 10 minutes closes the robot positions (R7).
16. A NOT_READY sleeve's position closes at its `LatestExit` only after the first READY check of the start and 10 minutes of NOT_READY (§4.8): up to 10 minutes after `LatestExit` when a symbol stays NOT_READY.
17. No order is sent in a shut minute (§4.1): if FP quotes crypto at weekends, or a market in a known closure, those quotes trade nothing. For a masked minute the paper agrees, since it never keeps such a bar. For a known closure it may not: the mask keeps gold bars that fall inside the built-in closures (in sample 89 bars on 22 holiday dates, e.g. 2019-07-04 13:00-16:56 New York; `check/out/clock/xau_holiday_scan.csv`), so if FP quotes gold there, the paper can fill where the robot sends nothing, and the robot writes `MISSED reason=market_shut`. The effect is a missed trade, never an unwanted order; no in-sample paper fill falls on, or one minute before, such a bar (fixes recheck R2, `build_reports_fixes.json`).
