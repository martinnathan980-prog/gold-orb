# The scan's grammar made exact (lead/DIRECTION4.md, section 2 and the stages of sections 3-5)

Written by the builder BEFORE any run on real bars that computes a return (the only real-bar pass so far is the
counts-only pass: signal counts before 2019-01-01, no price move after a signal, no return, no trade). Where
DIRECTION4 is exact, the code follows it; where it is not, the detail fixed here is the usual textbook meaning, chosen
from the text alone, never from a result. The rule list is `scan/rules.csv` (generated by `scan/grammar.py`; its sha256
is printed by `python3 -B -m scan.grammar`).

## 1. Bars, frames and the session clock
- **Bars**: the kit's hourly BID bars (`kit.data.load_bars`, FP costs; oil with its provisional costs and its
  roll-adjusted bars), indexed by the bar's open (UTC). An hourly bar's values are known at open + 1 h.
- **Session clock** = New York wall time + 7 h (17:00 New York = 00:00 of the clock), as `kit.data.trading_day`.
  Daylight saving follows New York (the switch happens on a Sunday at 02:00 New York, inside the weekend closure).
- **D1** = one day of that clock (17:00 to 17:00 New York). **H4** = one of its six 4-hour blocks: 17-21, 21-01, 01-05,
  05-09, 09-13, 13-17 New York. An hourly bar belongs to the block holding its open. OHLC: first open, highest high,
  lowest low, last close of the block's hourly bars; a block without any hourly bar does not exist (closures: the
  frame is the sequence of existing blocks; indicators run over that sequence, closures are not bars).
- **known_at of an H4 / D1 bar** = the scheduled end of its block (17:00, 21:00, 01:00, 05:00, 09:00, 13:00 New York).
  For a block whose last hourly bar ends at the block's end (every block of the round-the-clock calendars) this is
  exactly "the end of its last hourly bar" of DIRECTION4. A block whose bars stop earlier (an early close, a data hole)
  cannot be known complete before its scheduled end (the next hourly bar could still belong to it), so it is known at
  its scheduled end: never before its last hourly bar has closed (asserted in `frames.build`), and never before it
  is certain the block is over (the truncation test would otherwise fail on it). This changes no trade against the
  text's stamp (the end of the last hourly bar) for an H4 or D1 rule: no hourly bar of the market opens between that
  end and the scheduled end (it would belong to the block), so the entry bar of section 6 is the same with either
  stamp, and so is the F1/F2 trend of an H4 / D1 signal (the H4 block holding a D1 block's last bar ends with it);
  tested on bars whose blocks stop early (`test_scan.py` (8): same trades for H4 / D1 rules with F0-F3). Two effects
  remain, both causal and left to the lead (finding FRAME-KNOWN): (a) an H1 signal on the LAST bar of an H4 block
  that stops early sees, for F1/F2, the previous H4 block (the stopped block is not yet known complete), where the
  text's stamp would let it see its own block; (b) the kit's null groups basket legs by known_at, so legs of one
  scheduled block end are drawn jointly. The kit publishes no hourly trading schedule per market, so a block cannot be
  known complete before its scheduled end without reading the next bar.
- **A build at time `now`** (`frames.build(h1, frame, now)`) emits only blocks whose scheduled end is <= now; bars
  ending after `now` are refused. Discovery builds with now = 2019-01-01 00:00 UTC (the wall): the D1 day and H4
  block running across the wall do not exist in discovery. Confirmation builds with now = 2022-01-01 00:00 UTC (the
  kit's period end).
- **H1** = the hourly bars themselves, known at open + 1 h.

## 2. Indicators (`scan/indicators.py`), warm-up
Every value at a bar uses that bar and earlier bars of the same frame only. NaN during the warm-up; a signal needs
valid inputs on its bar (and on the previous bar when it is a crossing or an onset, below).
| indicator | exact definition | first valid bar (0-based) |
|---|---|---|
| SMA(n) | mean of the last n values | n - 1 |
| EMA(n) | alpha 2/(n+1), seeded with the SMA of the first n values | n - 1 |
| Wilder(n) | alpha 1/n, seeded with the SMA of the first n values | n - 1 of its input |
| TR | max(h - l, abs(h - c_prev), abs(l - c_prev)) | 1 |
| ATR(n) | Wilder(n) of TR from bar 1 | n |
| RSI(n) | 100 - 100/(1 + Wilder(n) gains / Wilder(n) losses) of the close changes; losses 0 -> 100 (50 if gains are 0 too) | n |
| MACD(12,26,9) | EMA12 - EMA26; signal = EMA9 of that line (seeded at the line's 9th valid value) | 25; signal 33 |
| +DI, -DI, ADX(14) | +DM = up move if > down move and > 0, else 0 (mirror for -DM); DI = 100 Wilder(DM)/ATR; DX = 100 abs(+DI - -DI)/(+DI + -DI) (0 if the sum is 0); ADX = Wilder(14) of DX | DI 14, ADX 27 |
| Bollinger(20, 2) | SMA20 +/- 2 x population sd (ddof 0) of the last 20 closes; width = (upper - lower) / middle | 19 |
| Keltner | EMA20 of the close +/- 2 x ATR20 (Wilder) | 20 |
| Stochastic(14,3,3) | fast %K = 100 (c - LL14)/(HH14 - LL14) (50 if HH = LL); %K = SMA3(fast %K); %D = SMA3(%K) | 17 |
| CCI(20) | TP = (h+l+c)/3; (TP - SMA20(TP)) / (0.015 x mean abs deviation of the 20 TP around that SMA); NaN if the deviation is 0 | 19 |
| SuperTrend(10, 3) | ATR10 (Wilder); basic bands hl2 +/- 3 ATR; final upper = basic upper if below the previous final upper or the previous close was above it, else the previous final upper (lower mirrored); direction starts +1 at the first ATR bar, turns -1 when the close is below the final lower band, +1 when above the final upper band | 10 |
| Ichimoku(9,26,52) | tenkan = mid of the 9-bar high/low range, kijun = 26-bar mid; the cloud AT bar i = (span A, span B) computed at bar i - 26: span A = (tenkan + kijun)/2, span B = 52-bar mid | 77 |
| Parabolic SAR(0.02, 0.2) | Wilder: start at bar 1, up if h1 + l1 >= h0 + l0, SAR = bar 0's extreme on the other side, EP = bar 1's extreme; then SAR += AF (EP - SAR), capped by the two previous lows (up) / highs (down); a bar whose low (high) reaches the SAR flips (SAR = old EP, EP = the bar's extreme, AF = 0.02); a new extreme adds 0.02 to AF up to 0.2 | 1 |
| ER(24) | abs(c - c[24]) / sum of the 24 abs(close changes) ending at the bar; NaN if the sum is 0 | 24 |
| IBS | (c - l)/(h - l); NaN if h = l | 0 |
| bar return | log(c / c_prev) | 1 |
| rolling sd | sample sd (ddof 1) of the window | window - 1 |

## 3. Signal conventions (`scan/signals.py`)
- A signal is +1 (buy) or -1 (sell) at the close of a frame bar and is known at that bar's known_at.
- **Crossing** of a above b at bar t: a_t > b_t and a_{t-1} <= b_{t-1} (strict now, weak before: touching is not a
  crossing, leaving a tie is); below: a_t < b_t and a_{t-1} >= b_{t-1}; all four values valid.
- **"On the bar where the condition becomes true" (onset)**: for a STATE condition (a level is exceeded, a pattern
  holds): true at t, false at t-1, and the inputs at t-1 valid (so no signal on the first valid bar). Applied to every
  state condition below (T06, T07, T09, T10, T11, R01-R03, R05, R07, R08, K01-K06, C01-C03, P01, P02, H02, H03, H04),
  per side. "First bar where it holds" (H03) is the same onset.
- **Events** (crossings, flips, "comes back", "rises above") fire where they happen: T01-T05, T08, T12, T13, R04, R06,
  K07, L01, H01, H05.
- A bar where both a buy and a sell fire gives no signal.
- Ties in thresholds: "<" and ">" are strict as written (RSI < 10, IBS > 0.8, range > 2.5 ATR, |z| > k ...), ">=" where
  written (ADX >= 25, 85 %).

| id | exact definition (buy; the sell is the mirror unless said) |
|---|---|
| T01-T03 | EMA(10) crosses above EMA(30); EMA(20)/EMA(50); EMA(50)/EMA(200) |
| T04 | MACD line crosses above its signal line |
| T05 | MACD line crosses above 0 |
| T06, T07 | onset of close > the highest high of the 20 (55) bars BEFORE this bar (sell: close < lowest low of the previous 20 / 55) |
| T08 | SuperTrend direction turns from -1 to +1 |
| T09 | onset of close > max(span A, span B) of the cloud at this bar (sell: close < min(span A, span B)); "leaves the cloud upward" = becomes above the cloud, from inside or from below |
| T10 | onset of close > EMA20 + 2 ATR20 (sell: close < EMA20 - 2 ATR20): follow |
| T11 | onset of close > upper Bollinger(20,2) (sell: < lower): follow |
| T12 | +DI crosses above -DI and ADX(14) >= 25 on that bar (sell: -DI crosses above +DI and ADX >= 25) |
| T13 | SAR direction turns from down to up |
| R01-R03 | onset of RSI(2) < 10 (sell > 90); RSI(7) < 20 (> 80); RSI(14) < 30 (> 70) |
| R04 | %K crosses above %D and %D < 20 on that bar (sell: %K crosses below %D and %D > 80) |
| R05 | as T11 with the sides reversed (fade) |
| R06 | CCI_t > -100 and CCI_{t-1} <= -100 (sell: CCI_t < 100 and CCI_{t-1} >= 100) |
| R07 | onset of c_t < c_{t-1} < c_{t-2} < c_{t-3} (three closes down in a row, strict): buy; three up: sell |
| R08 | onset of IBS < 0.2 buy / IBS > 0.8 sell; H4 and D1 only |
| K01, K02 | z_t = bar return_t / sd(the 500 bar returns t-500 .. t-1); onset of z > 3 (4): buy; z < -3 (-4): sell (follow) |
| K03, K04 | the same, sides reversed (fade) |
| K05, K06 | onset of (h - l) > 2.5 x ATR(20) of the PREVIOUS bar (the shock bar's own range is kept out of the ATR it is compared with) with c > o: buy (K05) / sell (K06); c < o mirrored; c = o: none |
| K07 | every bar s with abs(z_s) > 3 (K01's score; every such bar, not only onsets) is a shock; level L = c_s - 0.382 (c_s - c_{s-1}) (38.2 % of the shock bar's close-to-close move); for an up shock the FIRST bar j in s+1 .. s+6 with l_j <= L and c_j > L gives a buy at j (down shock: h_j >= L and c_j < L, sell); at most one signal per shock |
| C01 | width = (upper - lower)/middle of Bollinger(20,2); a bar is "lowest" if its width <= the minimum of the 120 widths ending at it; onset of [one of bars t-6 .. t-1 is lowest, all six with a defined 120-bar minimum] and close > upper (buy) / < lower (sell) |
| C02 | bar t-1 is NR7 (range_{t-1} <= the minimum of the 7 ranges t-7 .. t-1, ties count); onset of [NR7 at t-1 and c_t > h_{t-1}] buy / [c_t < l_{t-1}] sell; needs t >= 7 |
| C03 | bar t-1 inside t-2: h_{t-1} <= h_{t-2}, l_{t-1} >= l_{t-2}, and a strictly smaller range; onset of [inside and c_t > h_{t-2}] buy / [c_t < l_{t-2}] sell |
| P01 | bullish engulfing: bar t-1 down (c < o), bar t up, o_t <= c_{t-1}, c_t >= o_{t-1}, body_t > body_{t-1}, and min(l_t, l_{t-1}) is the lowest low of the 10 bars ending at t; bearish mirrored with the highest high: sell |
| P02 | bullish pin: range > 0, lower wick (min(o,c) - l) >= 2 x body and >= 0.6 x range, and l_t is the lowest low of the 10 bars ending at t: buy; bearish: upper wick, highest high: sell; onset |
| L01 | see section 5 |
| H01 | ER_t > 0.5 and ER_{t-1} <= 0.5; side = sign(c_t - c_{t-24}) (follow the 24-bar move) |
| H02 | onset of [ER < 0.15 and c_t = the lowest close of the 24 closes ending at t]: buy; highest: sell (fade) |
| H03 | k = number of the last 20 closes above the EMA20 of their own bar; onset of k >= 17 (85 %): buy; k <= 3 (15 %): sell |
| H04 | onset of IBS > 0.8 on bars t, t-1, t-2: buy; IBS < 0.2 on the three: sell |
| H05 | z_t = log(c_t / c_{t-24}) / (sd x sqrt 24), sd = sample sd of the 500 bar returns BEFORE the 24-bar move (t-523 .. t-24); z rises above +3 (z_t > 3, z_{t-1} <= 3): buy; falls below -3: sell |

Signals run on all three frames, R08 on H4 and D1 only, L01 on every market but oil (roll-adjusted bars).

## 4. Filters (the mixes)
- F0 none.
- F1 / F2: trend = sign(close - EMA200 of the closes) of the LAST H4 bar known at or before the signal's known_at
  (equality counts: a D1 signal known at 17:00 New York sees the 13-17 block). F1: side = trend; F2: side = -trend;
  trend 0 or undefined (warm-up of the EMA200: 200 H4 bars) -> the signal is in neither.
- F3: ADX(14) of the signal's own frame, at the signal bar, >= 25. F4: < 20 (undefined -> neither).
  Consequence: T12 (which requires ADX >= 25) with F4 has no signal by construction (6 rules, kept in the list as
  generated: the grammar is the full product).
- F5 / F6 / F7 (H1 only): the New York hour of the signal bar's OPEN: F5 Asia 18..23, 0..2; F6 Europe 3..7; F7 US
  8..16. The 17:00 New York bar belongs to no session.

## 5. Round levels (L01)
Grid: 0.01 (eurusd, gbpusd, audusd, nzdusd, usdcad, usdchf), 1.00 (usdjpy, eurjpy), 10 (xauusd), 0.50 (xagusd),
100 (us100, ger40), 50 (us500), 500 (us30); not on oil. Cell k_t = floor(c_t / grid), with c/grid rounded to 9
decimals first (a close on a level counts as at or above it). The move from k_{t-1} to k_t crosses the levels m x grid
for k_{t-1} < m <= k_t (up) or k_t < m <= k_{t-1} (down). L01 fires (follow: up = buy, down = sell) when at least one
crossed level was crossed by no close-to-close move of the 120 previous bars (moves ending at t-120 .. t-1). Needs
t >= 120 (a full 120-bar history in the frame).

## 6. Exits, stop, entry (`scan/engine.py`)
- `kit.sim.simulate` with mode 'one' (one position per market and rule), exit ('hours', N): H1 6 and 24, H4 24 and 72,
  D1 48 and 120; stop ('d20', 2.0); no target; the kit's FP costs: net, stress (spread and slippage x1.5) and gross,
  from the same signals (the kit decides which signals are taken on the net scenario).
- Entry at the open of the first hourly bar opening at or after known_at (delay 0), **with no time limit**
  (`max_wait_h=None`), as DIRECTION4 section 2 is written ("A signal known at a bar's end enters at the open of the
  next hourly bar"): a signal known while the market is shut enters at the reopening, however long the closure (a
  Friday D1 or 13-17 H4 signal on FX and metals at the Sunday reopening; an index signal of the session close at the
  next session's open; after a data hole at the first bar after it). A signal with no later bar (the end of the bars,
  the wall in discovery) has no trade (the kit's 'no_bar'). (Corrected after review, finding ENTRY-WAIT: an earlier
  version dropped signals whose next bar opened more than 2 hours later, which DIRECTION4 does not say: about 20 % of
  the D1 signals on FX and metals and half of those on the indices.)
- One simulate call per market and exit length holds every rule of that exit, each with its own `rule` label; the
  one-position filter, D20, costs and skips are the kit's.
- Aggregates per rule and market: daily sums of net R, R at costs x1.5 and gross R and the number of trades per entry
  trading day (17:00 New York, `kit.costs.trading_day`); per calendar year of entry (UTC, as `kit.stats.by_year`): n,
  sums of positive and of negative net and stress R (for PF), sums of R, R_stress, R_gross.

## 7. Stage 1, discovery (`scan/discover.py`)
- The reader sets the kit's period end to the wall (2019-01-01 00:00 UTC) while reading (`kit.data.load_bars` filters
  the prepared files inside the read: no row of 2019 or later is read), then cuts the bars whose open is >= the wall
  (each kept bar ends at or before it) and asserts it. `run_market` refuses bars reaching the wall; `sim.Book` is built
  under the same period end (it refuses a bar at or after it). Every trade is entered before the wall; a trade whose
  exit would fall after the last bar is dropped by the kit (open_at_end).
- One file per market (`<market>.npz`) written atomically, with a digest of its tables and the hash of the run's
  manifest (rules sha256, wall, source, subset); a restart skips a market whose file is complete and consistent, and
  redoes a missing or broken one; the tables are deterministic (same digests with 1 or 2 processes).
- `--counts-only` writes, per rule and market, the number of signals after the filter and the number with a later bar
  to enter on (bar times only); no Book, no trade, no return.

## 8. Stage 2a, selection (`scan/select.py`, section 4)
- Units: ALL15, FX8, IDX4, COM3, and each market alone. **A unit's markets for a rule** = the unit's markets the rule
  runs on (L01: ALL15 has 14, COM3 has gold and silver).
- n = trades; PF = sum of positive net R / abs(sum of negative net R) over trades (no loss: inf; no trade: 0); t =
  `kit.stats.t_stat` of the unit's daily sums of net R (sum over its markets per entry trading day; days with at least
  one trade; NaN with fewer than 2 days); PF at costs x1.5 the same on R_stress; positive years = calendar years
  2013-2018 with a positive sum of net R (a year without a trade is not positive); share of markets positive = markets
  of the unit with a positive total net R / the unit's markets (a market without a trade is not positive); largest
  market share = the largest market total / the unit total, failing when the unit total is <= 0.
- Eligible exactly as section 4 (n >= 200 basket / 150 market; PF >= 1.10; PF x1.5 >= 1.02; >= 4 positive years; a
  basket: share of markets positive >= 0.65 (COM3: 2 of 3 = 0.667 passes, 1 of 3 fails) and largest share <= 0.50,
  0.35 for ALL15 and FX8); the sibling bar: max t over the rule's siblings on the same unit >= 1.0.
- **Siblings** (`grammar.siblings`): the same signal, frame and filter with the other exit of the frame; and the
  neighbouring parameter(s) of the same family with the same frame, filter and exit, along these chains only (the
  families' parameters in DIRECTION4): T01-T02-T03 (EMA pairs), T06-T07 (20/55), R01-R02-R03 (RSI 2/7/14), K01-K02
  (follow 3/4 sd), K03-K04 (fade 3/4 sd). Other signals have only the other-exit sibling (K05/K06, T10/T11, R05 are
  different conditions or directions, not parameters).
- Picks: eligible baskets with t >= 2.5 in order of t (descending; ties by rule index, then unit order ALL15, FX8,
  IDX4, COM3, then markets as listed), at most 12; then single markets with t >= 3.0, at most 12. **Correlation**:
  Pearson of the two daily result series = the unit's net R summed per trading day over every business day of
  2013-01-01 .. 2019-01-01 (zeros kept); a candidate correlating above 0.6 with any one already picked (baskets and
  single markets) is skipped (a constant series has no correlation and is not skipped).
- Output `frozen_candidates.json` (sorted keys, rule, unit, markets, discovery figures, discovery manifest and per-market
  digests), its sha256 printed and written to `frozen_candidates.sha256`; an existing different list is never
  overwritten. No pick: "nothing" and the scan stops (confirmation then has nothing to compute).

## 9. Stage 2b, confirmation (`scan/confirm.py`, section 5)
- The wall, checked before anything is computed (corrected after review, finding CONFIRM-NOT-BOUND): the list's
  sha256 must equal the hash on the command line, which is the hash the lead committed (`run_all.sh` takes it from
  FROZEN_SHA only, never from `frozen_candidates.sha256`); its rules_sha256 must be the sha256 of `scan/rules.csv`
  (itself the generated list); every candidate must be a rule of rules.csv with its index, on a grammar unit, with
  markets = the unit's markets the rule runs on; and the selection recomputed from the discovery directory
  (`select.frozen_text`) must give the list byte for byte (so its manifest, per-market digests, figures and picks are
  those of the discovery files). Confirmation runs once per list: `confirmation.json` of another list is never
  replaced (refused), a second run of the same list must reproduce it exactly (else refused, the first kept), and
  every run, refused or not, is appended to `confirmation_runs.log`.
- Only the listed rules are computed (only their signal-frame; the H4 frame is built for the F1/F2 trend).
- Bars 2013-2021 (the kit's lock: before 2022-01-01); signals and simulation exactly as in discovery (indicators warm
  up on the earlier years, the one-position state runs from 2013); judged = trades ENTERED in 2019-01-01 .. 2021-12-31.
- Bars: t on daily sums >= 1.65; PF >= 1.08; PF x1.5 >= 1.00; >= 2 positive years of 2019-2021; a basket: share of
  markets positive >= 0.60; `kit.stats.null_test` (2,000 draws, the rule's own exit and stop, default mode 'every',
  joint same-day draws for legs with the same known_at) percentile >= 90. The null's books hold the bars from
  2019-01-01 on, so the random days are confirmation days (the first ~20 trading days of 2019 have no D20 and are not
  drawn). The same-year null (`within='year'`) is reported as information.

## 10. Reproducibility
`run_all.sh` runs discovery (3 processes) and selection, then stops until the lead has committed
`frozen_candidates.json`; confirmation runs with FROZEN_SHA = that committed hash (refused without it), then the
report; logging to `~/explore_data/scan/run.log`. Each step is safe to start again (discovery resumes, selection
refuses to overwrite a different frozen list, confirmation reruns only the same list and must reproduce its
result, the report is recomputed identically).

## 11. Discovery summary (`scan/report.py`)
Shares of rule-units with t > 2 and t < -2 by family, frame and unit over every rule-unit (DIRECTION4 section 6), and
beside them the same shares and the median t over the rule-units with n >= 30 trades (a t on a handful of trades is
huge and noisy: no mean or sd of t is reported). The summary carries notes on the choices of this file that the
reader should know (entry without time limit, the known_at stamp, K05-K07, siblings, F1/F2, the 500-bar warm-up of
K01-K04, K07, H05 on D1 which leaves about 4 usable discovery years).
