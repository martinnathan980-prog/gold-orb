TRACK ml (round 2) -- plan written BEFORE the first trial (2026-11-10)

Research universe (fixed a priori, one market per asset group): us100, ger40, xauusd, btcusd.
  us500/us30 are near-copies of us100 (no independent evidence); xagusd (5.4 bp spread = 12 % of an hourly range)
  and ethusd (6.5 bp, data from 2018 only) are left out a priori.

Candidate generator (fixed a priori, same on every market):
  H1 BID bars, session bars only = bars starting 02:00..15:00 New York, Mon-Fri (the only hours present in every
  year of the index data: 2015-03..2018-03 has no overnight quotes). Unit A = mean (high-low) of the last 70
  session bars. Candidate = H1 close above the highest high / below the lowest low of the previous n=14 session
  bars, decisions 03:00..15:00 NY. Market entry on the next M1 bar. No overnight position.

Stage A (8 trials): unfiltered generator, two exit variants x 4 markets
  X1: stop 2.0 A, hold to 15:55 NY          X2: stop 1.5 A, time exit after 4 h (capped at 15:55 NY)
  Exit kept = higher mean net R per candidate (every candidate taken, equal weight per market).

Stage B (walk-forward, pooled model over the 4 markets, one threshold for all markets):
  label = net R of each candidate taken on its own (engine, FP costs); folds: train on candidates decided before
  (1 Jan Y - 5 days), predict year Y, Y = 2016..2021 (btcusd candidates exist from 2017-05).
  B1 ridge regression on R (clipped to [-2, 4]), 13 numeric features + 3 time-of-day dummies + 2 weekday dummies,
     alpha fixed a priori; threshold = top 50 % of the training-fold scores (q=0.5)
  B2 same model, q=0.25                      B3 same model, threshold "predicted R >= 0"
  B4 logistic regression on (R>0)            B5 boosted trees depth 2, 40 trees, lr 0.05, min leaf 200
  B6 reduced feature set
  Each variant = 4 trials (one per market). Model choice by pooled out-of-fold result; cross-market consistency
  is part of the judgement.
Stage C: 1-2 threshold hand rules built from the model's strongest features (thresholds set on training folds);
  if a rule does as well as the model, the rule is preferred.
Stage D: at most 2 finalists, full criteria, neighbours, null test, permutation test, truncation test,
  finalists_preval.json, then one validation run per sleeve.
Not used as features (a priori): trade side (would let the model learn "the market went up"), spread level
  (the FP frame's spread is a model, and the cost-stress test would change the score), round-number distance
  (market-specific grids), result of the previous candidate (needs a virtual-trade tracker in the EA; the
  cheaper proxies seq/opp are used instead).
