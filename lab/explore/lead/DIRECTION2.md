# Direction 2 of the open search (lead, 2026-11-18)

Why: by 18 November the search had handled 17 ideas (12 run to a verdict, none passed; 5 closed or blocked before a
run). Almost all show no gross edge: unusual signals held a few days on the lab's 16 markets, and pairs fading a
stretch, do not pay. The user's instruction of 18 November: "if my directions are poor, change direction; you know my
goal". What the lab's own record says works: momentum and breakouts on volatile markets (the ten rules). What it never
tested: single shares around their earnings, holds longer than five days, the working rules on markets outside the 16.

Three new grounds, in this order of priority:
1. **Company earnings on US share CFDs** (about 40 large shares with one-minute history from 2017 on the lead's
   machine; earnings times from SEC EDGAR 8-K item 2.02, the filing's acceptance time).
2. **Slow rules** (holds up to 40 trading days; CHARTER "Slow track").
3. **The working families on new markets** (oil first; Nikkei, silver, natural gas later), with PROVISIONAL costs
   until the broker's own figures are read in the user's MetaTrader.
The scout's remaining ideas stay in the queue; the nature oddities (weather, moon, air, geomagnetic storms) are parked.

## Changes of 2026-11-18 (after the preparation, before any run of these ideas)
- The preparation (kit v2.1 and the earnings calendar, each attacked by an independent verifier) changed four briefs:
  **053** is rewritten on an expected date (a release date cannot be shown public in advance from the reachable
  sources); **054** reads the gap at 10:00 New York (the first bar's own open is unreliable in about 3 % of sessions);
  **055** drops the two crypto markets (reasons in its row); **056** and **057** run on oil bars that the kit now
  back-adjusts for the monthly contract roll (the raw series jumped at each roll: a long held across a roll booked a
  fake gain).
- **058** is new (the slow rule of 055 on the shares).
- Dropped before being written as ideas, because the lab had already closed them (checked in the lab's own records,
  not re-run): the opening rules noise30 and ocm4 on single shares (`lab/r2/tracks/stocks`: gross +2 bp a trade, net
  -10 bp: costs are five times the edge); the ten rules carried unchanged to every other market of the lab
  (`lab/r2/tracks/autopsy/breadth`: 126 rule-market pairs; the two best went to the validation look and failed).
  What that study could NOT run is the open ground kept here: oil (its data had the roll jumps) and any rule holding
  a share overnight.
