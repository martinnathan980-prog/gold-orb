Track "run" - list of ideas written BEFORE any engine run (2026-11-11). In-sample only (< 2022-01-01).
Common frame: gold, signals on mid prices, 15-minute bars + Bollinger(20,2), levels from the previous full trading day
(17:00 New York), dATR = 14-day mean true range. Core levels = R1, S1, R2, S2, yesterday's high and low
(chosen from the first-pass table: these are the levels that break more than placebo; P does not).
Baseline exits: stop 0.25 dATR behind the broken level, target = next daily pivot, forced exit 16:50 New York.
