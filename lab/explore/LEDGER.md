## Notes for later runs (the lead, 2026-11-18)
- **GDELT 1.0 daily event files** are written at about 07:00 UTC on D+1 since 2015-03, between 07:34 and 14:15 UTC from
  2014-02 to 2015-02, and the days of 2013-04 to 2014-01 were back-processed in February 2014 (not as first
  published). A rule on these files enters at the 08:00 UTC bar of D+1 or later. Evidence:
  `ideas/002_news_tone/gdelt_last_modified.txt`.
- **Wikimedia page views** answer HTTP 429 to bursts from the cloud's shared address: one request every 10 seconds or
  slower. **CFTC (Socrata)** gives the whole history in one request. **FINRA short-volume files**: about 3,900 small
  files came through with 2 threads and pauses. **FRED and Yahoo** answer the kit's readers.
- **Closest miss on record: idea 025** (long the four indices from the Monday to the Friday of options-expiry week):
  PF 1.42 net, 7 of 9 years, null at the 99.6th percentile, correlation with the ten 0.00, but t on daily sums 1.65
  against 2.5 (the four indices are one bet) and both 2020 and 2021 negative. Closed; testing a variant of it on the
  same years would be tuning.
- **Kit, fixed on 2026-11-18 (kit v2):** hedged pairs, exits on a condition (which also express "exit on a given
  weekday and hour"), crypto venues before 2017-08, a same-day null for baskets whose legs are decided together
  (the plain null was too easy for them: ideas judged as baskets before this date all failed anyway), no duplicate
  rows in `trials.jsonl` on a re-run. **Still open:** `data.external` keeps one row per known-at time (build an
  event calendar by hand with `data.known_at`); no exchange holiday calendar; early-2013 index bars are sparse (some
  signals dropped as `no_d20`); a rule whose exit differs by leg needs one `simulate` call per leg.
- **Batch of 2026-11-18 (ideas 051 to 058, kit v2.1 after three reviews, every figure re-run by the lead):**
  - **Slow trends give nothing before costs** (055 on 14 FP markets: PF gross 1.04; 058 on the 40 shares: below a
    random entry on the same share and side). Swaps then take 19 to 49 bp a trade.
  - **Earnings on the 40 shares:** buying before the expected date is the shares' own rise (053, null at the 48th
    percentile); following the move during the results day loses even gross (054). The five-session drift (052) has a
    gross edge (PF 1.31, +32 bp a trade) eaten by about 30 bp of costs. Read by size AFTER the verdict (a hypothesis,
    not a finding): nil below 5 %, +133 bp gross at 8 % or more (80 trades, t 2.5 gross, every year positive).
    Direction 3 tests that on other shares.
  - **Oil:** both rules (056 channel breakout, 057 inventory report) earn on the short side only, in the crash years
    2015, 2018 and 2020; 056 reads PF net 1.22 with t 1.4 and a losing every-signal variant.
  - **The random-entry null alone says little** for a rule with event exits (056: 99.9th percentile with t 1.4) or
    with costs that are large against its stop (057: 97.6th with t 0.1): read t first.
  - **Kit limits met:** `('bdays', N)` ignores exchange holidays (052: 36 of 355 trades left a session early; a
    `('sessions', N)` exit comes with kit v2.2); event exits are per market, not per side or per trade, so a channel
    exit that differs by side or a trailing stop is written as a state machine and the null runs the same events;
    no exit "at the close N sessions later" (the last hour is given up); the calendar with `drop_late` leaves Alibaba
    without a row (the share basket has 39 names on results days).
  - **Count a threshold before writing it in a brief:** idea 051's 2 % threshold kept 226 weekends of 469, under the
    250 a single market needs (the lead's estimate was wrong).
