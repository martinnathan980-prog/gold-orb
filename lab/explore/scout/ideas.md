# Ideas for the open search, ranked (scout, 2026-11-17)

36 ideas in 11 families, best first. Nothing here was tested: no return, no look at prices after a signal. The counts in
the last column come from the signal data alone (for price-built signals: the lab's bars read with `ts < 2022-01-01`,
counting crossings only). Data sections refer to `sources.md` in this folder, which gives for each source the tested
fetch code and, above all, **when each value is known**. No idea uses a signal source the scout did not reach; prices are the charter's (Dukascopy, Binance), and Dukascopy
reset the scout's three test connections during the probe (see `sources.md`, blocked list).

How to read the table
- Times: "NY" is New York wall-clock time; otherwise UTC. Hourly bars are Dukascopy's (UTC hours).
- z(x, N) = (x - mean of the N previous values) / their standard deviation; the current value is never in the window.
- D20 = mean daily high-low range over the 20 completed trading days before the entry; the stop distance is the
  risk unit R of the trade.
- "Cash session": 09:30 to 16:00 New York for the US indices, 09:00 to 17:30 Berlin for ger40; no position is held
  overnight in such a rule, so no swap.
- A pair is two legs of equal notional at entry, both legs paying costs and swaps.
- Prior, in words: **very low** = under about 3 % to pass every in-sample bar and stay independent of the ten;
  **low** = about 3 to 8 %; **moderate** = about 8 to 15 %. For scale, the lab gave its own best picks of round 4
  between 7 and 15 % in sample, and "nothing passes" stays the likely outcome of any single run.
- "Live" says what the robot would need beyond MetaTrader if the rule were ever traded (lesson 15 of the lab:
  rules that depend on outside data are harder to deploy).

One ruling is needed before the pair ideas (1, 3, 4, 12, 24) are declared: the basket bars ask that no market make
more than a given share of the profit, which has no meaning for the two legs of a hedge (one leg can carry all the
profit while the other loses). The scout's suggestion: judge a hedged pair as ONE synthetic market, and several pairs
as a basket of pairs.

## Considered and left out (with the reason)
- **Swiss National Bank sight deposits, weekly (sources.md 9)**: only 124 weeks in 2013-2021 move by 1 billion francs
  or more (110 up; 36 of them in 2020, 22 in 2016, 2 in 2018): under 250 on one market by construction.
- **Earthquakes near Japan and the yen (sources.md 27)**: 231 events of magnitude 5.5 or more, 68 of 6 or more.
- **VIX above its 3-month sister**: 184 days in 42 episodes (406 days in 61 episodes above a 0.95 ratio): the nine-day
  version (idea 23) has more. **VIX up on a day the index is up**: 41 clear cases, 239 at the loosest definition.
- **Exchange inflows of bitcoin (Coin Metrics)**: 94 spike days, and the history is not point-in-time.
- **Funding rates around the 8-hourly marks**: BitMEX is blocked (403), Binance funding files start in 2020 and
  Bitfinex's long/short counts in 2021: under 4 in-sample years. Idea 28 is what the reachable data allows.
- **Ideas that need Yahoo** (GBTC premium, gold miners against gold, credit ETFs against shares, equal-weight against
  cap-weight, MOVE): Yahoo refused the first batch and the retry was not allowed in this session; not verified, not
  proposed. They become available the day `yahoo_daily` is seen to work in a run.
- **Gold and silver ETF holdings**: the SPDR archive URL now returns a PDF; not reached.
- **AAII, geopolitical risk (daily), San Francisco Fed news sentiment, term premium**: alive, but each is one binary
  file with the whole history to today, which cannot be cut at 2021 before it is downloaded (sources.md).
- **Treasury cash balance, daily swings**: known a day late; the predictable part is idea 32 (settlement days).
- **Primary dealers' positions (New York Fed)**: 365 weeks, eight days late.
- **Retail positioning at Dukascopy, Google Trends**: not reachable.
- **Daylight and winter blues, clock-change Mondays, Friday the 13th, eclipses, football results**: published, but 2
  to 18 events a year or a slow seasonal dummy: no power under the 250-trade and 5-day bars.
- **Turn of the month on the indices**: already looked at by the overnight track (close to close, night and cash
  session of days -1 to +3 on three indices): "shows nothing in-sample" (results/r2/overnight/candidates.json).
- **Quarter ends** (36), **pre-holiday sessions** (dropped by round 4 on count), **Shanghai gold benchmark times**
  (round 1 tested the Beijing clock on gold).
- **Quote-side volume from Dukascopy's bid and ask candles**: Dukascopy reset every connection during the probe and
  the meaning of the two volumes is not verified.

## A stronger design the lead may prefer
Ideas 1, 3 and 4 are one rule (fade a 20-day stretch of the ratio of two related markets) on seven pairs. Declared as
ONE basket of seven pairs it has about 1,300 trades and can use the ordinary basket bars; declared separately each
part is clearer to read but thinner. They should not be run both ways (that would be the same data tested twice).
