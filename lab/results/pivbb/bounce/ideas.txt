Common defaults unless stated: daily Traditional pivots P,R1-R3,S1-S3 from the previous full day; touch = events.py logic
(armed after a close 0.15 dATR away); FADE the touch; market entry at the open of the next minute; stop 0.25 dATR beyond the
level (or beyond the extreme since the touch for confirmation entries); target 1R; forced exit 16:50 New York.

A. blind touch (4)
 A1 limit order at the level (optimistic fill)          A2 market next minute
 A3 market, target = 15-min middle band (min 0.5R)      A4 market, target = previous daily pivot (min 0.5R)
B. confirmation instead of a blind touch (8)
 B1 M5 bar closes back on the arrival side (within 3 bars)       B2 same on M15 (within 2 bars)
 B3 M5 rejection wick (wick >= 50 % of the bar's range)          B4 same on M15
 B5 M15 engulfing (a bar closes beyond the far end of the bar that touched)   B6 same on M5
 B7 failure to hold: M1 close >= 0.05 dATR beyond the level, then an M1 close back inside within 15 min
 B8 failure to hold within 60 min (M5 closes)
C. repeated touches and confluence (7)
 C1 first touch of the level today only     C2 second or later touch only
 C3 daily pivot within 0.10 dATR of a weekly / monthly pivot     C4 within 0.10 dATR of yesterday's high / low
 C5 within $1.5 of a $10 round number        C6 any confluence (W/M/PDH/PDL) + B1 confirmation
 C7 the weekly and monthly pivots themselves as levels
D. session and low volatility (7)
 D1 Asia (18:00-03:00 NY)   D2 Asia + 15-min band narrower than usual (squeeze < 1)   D3 range day: range used < 0.5 dATR and squeeze < 1
 D4 London morning (03-08 NY)   D5 New York morning (08-12)   D6 afternoon (12-17)   D7 Asia, target = middle band
E. exhaustion (8)
 E1 level beyond the 1-hour band (stretch60 >= 1)   E2 stretch60 >= 1.5   E3 fast approach (mom4 >= 2 ATR15 in the last hour)
 E4 slow approach (mom4 < 0.5)   E5 RSI divergence (new extreme vs the last 3 h, RSI lower than at the previous extreme)
 E6 level beyond the 15-min band and RSI >= 70 (the classic fade)   E7 band walk (>= 3 of last 8 closes outside)   E8 day's range >= 1.2 dATR already used
F. the user's asymmetric version: fade AGAINST a stretched move that arrives at a pivot beyond the outer band (6)
 F1 stretch15 >= 1, market, target middle band   F2 stretch15 >= 1 + M5 close back inside the band and inside the level, target middle band
 F3 stretch15 >= 1.5 and mom4 >= 2, + M5 confirmation, target middle band   F4 F2 with a wide stop (0.5 dATR)   F5 F2 with a tight stop (0.15 dATR)
 F6 F2 with classic roles only (R levels from below, S levels from above)
G. exits on the best rule of A-F (5). "Best" = highest PF under costs x1.5 among rules with n >= 250 (fixed now).
 G1 stop 0.15   G2 stop 0.50   G3 time stop 60 min   G4 time stop 240 min   G5 no target (stop or end of day)
H. model (1): walk-forward gradient boosting (train on years < Y) on the context of M5-confirmed touches, predicting the fade's result;
   trade its top fifth out-of-fold. Only taken to the engine if the out-of-fold top fifth is positive after costs at event level.
Neighbours for at most 2 finalists: up to 8 each (counted in the 80). Level-placebo runs and null tests of a finalist are verification, logged separately.
Finalist rule (fixed now): a configuration must pass r2.is_criteria in its MARKET-entry version; otherwise no finalist.
